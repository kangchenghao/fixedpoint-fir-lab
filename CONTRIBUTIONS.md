# Project ownership and contributions

## Project owner: kangchenghaokang-dot

I initiated and sustained the broader research exploration around computing
architecture and brain-related signal processing. I set its goals and practical
constraints, provided the surrounding hardware-project context, and asked for
comparisons that could support an assessable graduation-research project.

I later changed the priority from pursuing only a TTA-specific innovation claim
to building useful, inspectable public artifacts from the available work. I
requested lightweight background execution and reproducible deliverables. This
repository follows those priorities through a small numerical-engineering
experiment that can run without an FPGA, a large toolchain or private data.

My direct contribution is project motivation, direction, scope and resource
decisions, and sustained iteration. The particular synthetic FIR experiment,
code, test construction and local execution were AI-assisted. The standard FIR
method is not claimed as my invention, and no unperformed board experiment is
credited to me.

## 项目主导与实际贡献

我发起并持续推进计算架构与脑科学计算相关探索，明确目标、资源限制和希望形成可评估研究成果的要求。周边硬件工程提供了问题背景；本仓库的具体源文件则是新写的独立数值实验，没有把旧工程直接打包成新的个人成果。

我随后主动调整方向：与其一直追逐TTA的创新标签，不如先把现有工作做成别人能复现、能检查、也能继续修改的公开作品。我要求轻量、后台运行和可复现交付。这个FIR实验将这些目标落实为不依赖板卡、大型工具链或私人数据的小项目。

我的直接贡献是研究动机、方向与范围选择、资源约束和持续推进；具体实验设计、代码、测试、文档及运行采用AI协作。标准FIR方法不是我的发明；文档中的后续学习任务也不算作已完成的个人实验。实现分工见[AI_USAGE.md](AI_USAGE.md)。
