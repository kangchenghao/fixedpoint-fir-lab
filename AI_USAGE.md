# Development assistance and responsibility

The project was initiated and directed by **kangchenghaokang-dot**, who set the broader research priorities, practical constraints and reproducible-delivery goals. [CONTRIBUTIONS.md](CONTRIBUTIONS.md) distinguishes those contributions from implementation and execution.

Code, documentation, test construction and local experiments used AI assistance. Scoped review suggestions were checked against the executable numerical contract; they are not independent human peer review or engineering certification.

The numerical claims rely on tests, explicit arithmetic, recorded experiments and the range argument. Maintainers remain responsible for understanding and correcting the result. The standard FIR method is not claimed as a new invention, and passing finite tests is not a proof of every possible implementation.

The repository excludes private account and authentication information, internal development conversations and original human recordings. Its numerical experiment runs locally without network access.
